How to Reach IUCAA, Pune

By Air
Pune Airport (PNQ)
Distance: ~14 km • Approx. 35–45 minutes

Pre-paid Taxi: Counter available inside the arrivals terminal hall.

App Cabs: Ola and Uber designated pick-up bays right outside the terminal.

Route: Via Airport Road → Yerwada → Sangamwadi → Sancheti Hospital → Ganeshkhind Road into Pune University campus.

By Train
Pune Railway Station (Pune Jn)
Distance: ~7 km • Approx. 20–25 minutes

Auto-Rickshaw: Pre-paid auto booth and metered autos available 24/7 outside Platform 1 exit.

App Cabs: Ola and Uber pick-up points at the main station portico.

Route: Via Sassoon Road → RTO → Sancheti Hospital → University Road to IUCAA.

By Bus / Local
Shivajinagar Station / Bus Stand
Distance: ~4 km • Approx. 10–15 minutes

Closest Transit Hub: Primary arrival point for intercity buses and Mumbai–Pune local trains.

Transit: Auto-rickshaws are readily available outside directly to University Main Gate / IUCAA (~₹70–₹100).

Via Expressway
Mumbai Airport (CSMIA - BOM)
Distance: ~150 km • Approx. 3.5–4 hours

Intercity Cabs: 24/7 shared or private AC taxis (KK Travels, Cool Cabs) drop passengers directly inside IUCAA via the Mumbai-Pune Expressway.

Express Trains: Trains from Mumbai CST / Dadar to Pune Junction (e.g. Deccan Queen, Pragati Express).
